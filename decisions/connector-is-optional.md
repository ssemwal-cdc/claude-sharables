# The connector is optional

**Rule.** State the connector as optional and say what it adds. Never describe the browser route as lacking something.

**Outcome protected.** An unprovisioned teammate starts the same day instead of waiting on IT.

**Argument.**

The sheet used to present the connector as a required step. That told unprovisioned teammates to wait on IT when they could have started that day.

What it adds is stated positively. Faster queries, and the purchase order and billing history cross-check.

That framing matches the skill rule of stating what was checked and never what was not.

**Evidence.**

- Changed 2026-08-19 at step 2 of `docs/onboarding.html`.
- The connector needs a second account. See F39, the connector needs another account.
- Nothing runs with Chrome closed. See F62, nothing runs without Chrome.
