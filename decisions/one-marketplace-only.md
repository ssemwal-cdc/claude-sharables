# Never create a second marketplace

**Rule.** Keep one marketplace. Put every plugin in `plugins/`.

**Outcome protected.** A teammate keeps the marketplace they added and its plugins.

**Argument.**

One person can register one marketplace per name.

A second marketplace would replace this one rather than sit beside it.

Removing a marketplace uninstalls its plugins, so a replacement costs every teammate their installs.

**Evidence.**

- Observed 2026-08-21. Removing the marketplace from the app menu uninstalls its plugins, and a re-add lands on current.

**Checks.** none
