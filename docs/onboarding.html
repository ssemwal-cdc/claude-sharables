Approval Plugin Setup

Two plugins that review your NetSuite and Procore approval queues, check the figures against the attached support, and let you approve or reject from one dashboard.

~15 minutes, once

What you get for the 15 minutes

Set it up once and the review happens without you. On whatever schedule you pick, it works through your approval queue before you've opened it — reading the attached support, re-deriving the math, and tying the figures back to the purchase order and the billing history.

  • Every item arrives with a verdict. Clear, or flagged with the specific figure that's wrong — so you're checking one line, not the whole document. A figure it could only read by OCR is never marked clear, even when the arithmetic ties: it's labelled read by OCR, not independently verified and left flagged for you.
  • Some come back as "not ready to review", and that's deliberate. An item with nothing to check against isn't approved, isn't rejected, and isn't given a verdict it hasn't earned. It tells you why — no support attached, the support is a scanned image, the link expired — so you know whether to chase the vendor or just look at it yourself.
  • It reads more than PDFs. Spreadsheets are read sheet by sheet, hidden ones included. Images and scanned pages are looked at rather than run through text extraction, which returns nothing useful for them.
  • The noise is gone. Most of a Procore queue is distribution-only. You see what's actually yours to action, and a count of what wasn't.
  • Approving is one click. Mark what you want from the dashboard, press Execute, and it drives the real buttons in your browser.
  • It never decides anything. A verdict is a recommendation. Nothing moves until you name the items yourself.

Before you start

  • The Claude desktop app — install it if you don't have it, signed in with your Compass account.
  • Google Chrome, plus a Claude browser extension you'll install into it in step 1. Having used Claude in a Chrome tab is not the same thing and doesn't count.
  • A Procore login, a NetSuite login, or both. Install only the plugins you need — Procore-only people skip step 2 entirely, and install one plugin instead of two in step 3.
1

Install the Claude Chrome extension everyone

Steps 1–2 · in your browser This is a Chrome extension you install. Not a website. It lets Claude click through Procore and NetSuite on your behalf. You never type instructions into Chrome — get it installed and signed in, then leave it alone.
Don't skip this because you've "used Claude in Chrome" before.

Opening claude.ai in a Chrome tab is not this. Neither is the Claude desktop app. What you need here is a separate browser extension, installed from the Chrome Web Store, that gives Claude the ability to operate your browser — open tabs, read pages, click buttons.

If you have never deliberately installed a Claude extension into Chrome, you don't have it, and nothing else in this guide will work until you do. Both plugins depend on it entirely.

How to be sure: look for the Claude icon in Chrome's toolbar, next to the address bar. No icon there means it isn't installed, whatever else you've used Claude for.

Both plugins drive your real browser session, so approvals route normally and the audit trail records you.

  1. Use Google Chrome. Not Edge, not Safari.
  2. Sign in to Chrome with your Compass profile — the profile icon at the top right of the window. Everything below has to happen inside that profile.
  3. Install the extension, still in that profile: Claude on the Chrome Web Store. You should end up clicking a blue Add to Chrome button and accepting the permissions prompt. If you don't see that button, you're either already installed or in the wrong profile.
  4. Click the extension and sign in again with your Compass login. Signing in to Chrome and signing in to the extension are two separate steps — you do both.
  5. In that same profile, sign in to Procore and NetSuite.
Done whenThere's a Claude icon in Chrome's toolbar, clicking it shows you signed in, and Procore and NetSuite both load in that profile without asking you to log in again.
2

NetSuite connector NetSuite only · optional

Only use Procore? Skip to step 3.

This step is optional. The NetSuite review works without it — connect it if you have it, and if you don't, go straight to step 3 and set it up later. Nothing here blocks you.

  1. Click your profile at the bottom left, then Settings → Connectors.
  2. Find NetSuite in the list and connect it.
  3. Not listed? You haven't been set up for it — see below. Carry on to step 3 regardless.
  4. If you did connect it: go back to NetSuite in Chrome and check whose account you're in. It must be your normal one — see below.
What it adds, if you have it

The review gets faster, and it gains one extra check: each bill is tied back to its funding purchase order and the engagement's billing history, which is what catches duplicate payments, a missing intermediate application, and billing past the committed amount. Verdicts from a connected run say so — they name the PO and the history they were checked against.

Everything else is the same either way. The figures, the attached support, the approvals — all of that runs through your browser and never touched the connector.

Your normal NetSuite login is not the same thing as connector access.

The connector runs on a separate, Claude-enabled NetSuite account — a second account issued on top of your usual one. Having a NetSuite login does not mean you have this, which is why people are surprised not to find it in the list.

Only ever been given one NetSuite account? Then you don't have it yet. Check your email for a Compass invitation to add the NetSuite MCP connector, and contact IT if there isn't one — but don't wait on it. Finish the setup and start using the plugin today; it picks the connector up on its own once you have it, with nothing to reconfigure.

Got both accounts? Keep the browser on your normal one when approving — that's whose name goes on the approval. Connecting the connector can leave the browser session on its account, and in the wrong one you'll see a queue that isn't yours, or a bill with no approve buttons.

Done whenEither the connector shows connected and NetSuite in Chrome shows your name and role — not the connector's — or you've established you don't have it and you're moving on to step 3.
Steps 3–6 · in the Claude desktop app Browser set-up is done. Switch to the desktop app now. Everything from here happens there. Chrome just needs to stay open and signed in — Claude drives it in the background while you work in the app.
3

Install the plugins

Two ways to do this — pick one. They do exactly the same thing.

Option A — in the desktop app

  1. Click your profile at the bottom left, then Settings → Plugins.
  2. Click Add, then Add marketplace.
  3. Enter the repo: ssemwal-cdc/claude-sharables
  4. Check that auto sync shows as checked before you add it. It should be on by default. This is what keeps you on the current version — see below.
  5. Add it. Both plugins now appear in the list.
  6. Install the ones you need — netsuite-approval-review, procore-open-items-review, or both.

Option B — in the terminal

Only if you already work in a command line. If that means nothing to you, use Option A — you're not missing anything.

claude plugin marketplace add ssemwal-cdc/claude-sharables
claude plugin install netsuite-approval-review@compass-claude-plugins
claude plugin install procore-open-items-review@compass-claude-plugins

You add the repo but install from the marketplace, which is why those two names differ. It looks like a typo; it isn't. Inside a Claude chat the same commands work as /plugin … instead — and if a chat says Run /reload-plugins to activate, run that there.

Done whenBoth plugins appear in your installed list. If they don't turn up in a new chat, restart your computer — that sorts it.
4

First run

Budget 10–20 minutes. This is the longest run you'll do and the only one that really needs you — after it, setup is done for good.

It pauses a few times to ask permission and waits for an answer, so it won't finish on its own. You don't have to sit and watch it: check back every few minutes, or leave it open on a second screen. Just don't wander off for hours — let it time out and you'll be starting this part over.

  1. Create a new chat.
  2. Switch the toggle just below the prompt box from Chat to Cowork.
  3. Set the model to Opus 5, High effort.
  4. Point it at any folder.
  5. Type the command below and hit Enter — don't describe it in your own words.
Run thisIt asks you to confirm
NetSuite
/netsuite-approval-double-check		 Which dashboard portlet holds your bills, and — if you connected one in step 2 — your NetSuite identity and which connector it's calling.
Procore
/procore-open-items-review		 Your company id, your change-risk tool id, and the cost field mapping.
It will ask permission to use each site — pick always, not just once. This is Claude asking, not Chrome. You'll see it for Procore, for NetSuite, and once more the first time it opens an attachment. Choose "just once" and you'll be asked again on nearly every action — and a review sitting on a prompt you aren't watching just looks frozen.
Where your work is saved. The folder you pick gets a NetSuite Approval Checks folder, a Procore Open Items folder, or both — created on the first run. Each holds your review history, the ids you confirm above, and the dashboard itself. Use the same folder every time — it's how a scheduled run knows whether it already ran today. Point at a different folder, or rename or delete this one, and the next run treats you as brand new and asks the setup questions over again.

Nothing is shared between people — your ids and state stay local to your install. After this first run, setup is done and you won't be asked any of it again.

Done whenA dashboard appears inline in the conversation, listing the items waiting on you. If a red banner appears across the top, the dashboard got cut off on the way to the chat — your figures are fine; say so and it re-renders a smaller version.
5

Using it day to day

For now, you start each review yourself — the same command from step 4. Once the schedule in step 6 is running, you don't: the review has already happened and the dashboard is waiting, provided your machine was on and Chrome open when it fired.

  1. Read the dashboard. Each item shows a verdict, the figures behind it, and a link to the record.
  2. Mark the ones you want to action. The buttons depend on the system — see below.
  3. Press Execute. That puts an instruction into the chat in one click.
  4. The approvals then run through your own browser session, one record at a time.
Marking an itemThe buttons you'll see
NetSuite Approve, With notes, or Reject.
Procore Whatever that item's workflow step offers — usually Approve or Yes, and Reject or Revise and Resubmit. There's no "With notes", and a negative response asks you for a reason.
Every approval it makes says so. Approvals carry the note Approved by Claude in the audit trail. Type your own note for an item and yours is used instead, word for word. Rejection reasons are never filled in for you — it stops and asks.
Done whenIt reports counts back — actioned, skipped as already done, and anything that stopped the batch.
6

Run it on a schedule

In that same chat, send this — with the prompt below pasted underneath it:

I'd like to make this run on a schedule. Pasted below is the entire prompt of
instructions. Ask me what frequency I want this to run — the first try each day,
the subsequent tries, and whether it's weekdays only or weekends as well.

Claude asks you those questions, packages it up, and gives you an Add to schedule button. Click it.

A reasonable answer: first try 11am, retries 2pm and 4pm, weekdays only.

It only runs while your computer is on and Chrome is open.

Claude works through your real browser session, so a scheduled run needs your machine awake, Chrome running, and you still signed in to Procore and NetSuite. Laptop shut at 11am and nothing happens — it doesn't queue up and run later.

That's what the extra fire times are for. If the 11am run misses because you were in a meeting with the lid down, the 2pm one catches it. And if 11am already succeeded, the later ones check the log, see the day is done, and stop without re-reviewing anything.

The prompt to paste — NetSuite

Run the daily NetSuite approval double-check.

FIRST — idempotency gate. Read `NetSuite Approval Checks/_netsuite_review_log.json`
in the connected workspace folder. If `lastCompletedRun` equals today's date, a run
already succeeded today — stop immediately and output only: "Approval check already
completed today at <time>. Nothing to do." Do not open Chrome. Do not re-review
anything. If the file is missing or unreadable, treat that as "no run today" and
continue.

Otherwise, invoke the `netsuite-approval-double-check` skill and follow it end to
end. The skill owns the whole procedure — the queue queries, how attachments are
read, the math and support-adequacy checks, the cross-checks, the state file, and
how the dashboard is published. Follow what the skill says, not what this task
remembers.

Hard constraints, restated because a scheduled run has nobody watching it:
- This is a REVIEW run. Never click Approve, Approve With Notes, or Reject. Those
  are only ever clicked on an explicit instruction naming specific documents,
  which a scheduled run by definition does not have.
- Never call ns_createRecord or ns_updateRecord. The connector is read-only.
- Review every item in the queue regardless of dollar amount.
- Leave each record's tab open so I can act on it.
- If NetSuite is not authenticated, stop and report that the session expired.
  Never attempt to log in.

On success, update the state file per the skill's schema, including today's date as
`lastCompletedRun` and the run time as `lastRunTime`.

Then report in chat with ONE line and nothing else:
  <n> pending · <n> flagged · dashboard updated
Add a second line only if something blocked the run. Do not put per-item verdicts
in chat.

If the run fails partway, do NOT set `lastCompletedRun` — leave it so the next
fallback window retries. Report what blocked it.

attachments are read, the cost and pay-application checks, the state file, and how
the dashboard is published. Follow what the skill says, not what this task remembers.

Hard constraints, restated because a scheduled run has nobody watching it:

  items, which a scheduled run by definition does not have.
- Every Procore API call is a GET. Never POST, PUT, PATCH or DELETE.
- Review every item I can actually action, regardless of dollar amount.
- Leave each reviewed record's tab open so I can act on it. Close scratch tabs.
- If Procore is not authenticated, stop and report that the session expired. Never
  attempt to log in.

On success, update the state file per the skill's schema, including today's date as
`lastCompletedRun` and the run time as `lastRunTime`.

Then report in chat with ONE line and nothing else:
  <n> awaiting you · <n> flagged · <n> skipped · dashboard updated
Add a second line only if something blocked the run. Do not put per-item verdicts

Done whenThe task fires and either posts a one-line summary, or says a run already completed today.

Keeping it updated

With auto sync on, you're done — there's nothing to run. Fixes ship whenever Shivam makes changes and they reach you on their own. They apply on your next computer restart, so in practice you just have them the following morning.

Forcing an update — in the terminal

Only if you need a fix right now, or auto sync got switched off.

  1. Press Win + R, type cmd, hit Enter. (On a Mac, open Terminal.)
  2. Copy and paste the lines below one at a time, pressing Enter after each.
claude plugin marketplace update compass-claude-plugins
claude plugin update netsuite-approval-review@compass-claude-plugins
claude plugin update procore-open-items-review@compass-claude-plugins

Then restart your computer.

Check the version actually moved. The first command reports success whether or not anything shipped — it only refreshes the catalog. Only a changed hash proves an update landed, and the second command needs the @compass-claude-plugins suffix or it says the plugin isn't found. It is; the bare name just isn't enough.

Four things that will confuse you

What you seeWhat it means
It stops and says the session expired Chrome was closed, or you were signed out of Procore or NetSuite. Reopen Chrome in your Compass profile, sign back in to both, and run it again. It never logs in for you.

What it will never do

  • Approve or reject on its own judgement. A verdict is a recommendation, nothing more.
  • Act on "approve everything clear." It requires named documents and will ask which.
  • Click without re-checking. It confirms the number, vendor and amount on the record immediately before each click.
  • Keep going after a problem. If an item can't be confirmed, the whole batch stops.
Questions, or something in here that doesn't match what you see — tell Shivam. The plugins update from ssemwal-cdc/claude-sharables; every push to main is a new version.
