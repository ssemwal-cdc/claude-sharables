Approval Plugin Setup

Two plugins that review your NetSuite and Procore approval queues, check the figures against the attached support, and let you approve or reject from one dashboard.

~25 min to set up then a 15–20 min first run once

What you get

Set it up once and the review happens without you. On whatever schedule you pick, it works through your queue before you've opened it — reading the support, re-deriving the math, tying figures back to the purchase order.

  • Every item arrives with a verdict. Clear, or flagged with the specific figure that's wrong — so you check one line, not the whole document.
  • The noise is gone. Most of a Procore queue is distribution-only. You see what's yours to action, and a count of what wasn't.
  • Approving is one click, and it never decides anything. Mark what you want, press Execute. A verdict is a recommendation; nothing moves until you name the items.
What else it handles

Spreadsheets and images, not just PDFs. Workbooks are read sheet by sheet, hidden ones included. Images and scanned pages are looked at rather than run through text extraction, which returns nothing useful for them.

Some items come back "not ready to review", deliberately. An item with nothing to check against isn't approved, isn't rejected, and isn't given a verdict it hasn't earned. It tells you why — no support attached, support is a scanned image, the link expired — so you know whether to chase the vendor or just look yourself.

A figure it could only read by OCR is never marked clear, even when the arithmetic ties. It's labelled read by OCR, not independently verified and left flagged for you.

Before you start

  • The Claude desktop app, signed in with your Compass account.
  • Google Chrome, plus a Claude browser extension you'll install in step 1. Having used Claude in a Chrome tab is not the same thing.
  • A Procore login, a NetSuite login, or both. Install only the plugins you need.
1

Install the Claude Chrome extension everyone

Step 1 · in your browser This is a Chrome extension you install. Not a website. It lets Claude click through Procore and NetSuite on your behalf. Get it installed and signed in, then leave it alone.
Don't skip this because you've "used Claude in Chrome" before.

Opening claude.ai in a tab is not this. Neither is the desktop app. You need a separate browser extension that lets Claude operate your browser — open tabs, read pages, click buttons.

How to be sure: look for the Claude icon in Chrome's toolbar, next to the address bar. No icon means it isn't installed, whatever else you've used Claude for.

  1. Use Google Chrome. Not Edge, not Safari.
  2. Sign in to Chrome with your Compass profile — profile icon, top right. Everything below happens inside that profile.
  3. Install the extension, still in that profile: Claude on the Chrome Web Store. You should click a blue Add to Chrome and accept the permissions prompt. No button means you're already installed, or in the wrong profile.
  4. Click the extension and sign in again with your Compass login. Signing in to Chrome and signing in to the extension are two separate steps.
  5. In that same profile, sign in to Procore and NetSuite.
  6. Test it. Open a normal Claude chat and type: Open Procore in Chrome and tell me the page title. It should open a tab and answer.
Done whenThat test came back with a real page title. If it didn't, stop here and fix the extension — nothing later in this guide works without it.
Why the test matters

The Claude icon being present proves the extension is installed. It does not prove Claude can drive the browser, which is what everything else depends on. Thirty seconds here saves finding out twenty minutes into step 4, which is the one step you can't resume cleanly.

Both plugins drive your real browser session, so approvals route normally and the audit trail records you.

Steps 2–5 · in the Claude desktop app Chrome is set up. Switch to the Claude desktop app now — everything from here happens there. Chrome just needs to stay open and signed in. Claude drives it in the background while you work in the app.
2

NetSuite connector optional · Procore only? skip to step 3

The NetSuite review works without this. Connect it if you have it; if you don't, go to step 3 and set it up later. Nothing here blocks you.

  1. In the Claude desktop app — not Chrome — click your profile at the bottom left, then Settings → Connectors.
  2. Find NetSuite in the list and connect it.
  3. Not listed? You haven't been set up for it. Carry on to step 3.
  4. Did connect it? Go back to NetSuite in Chrome and check you're in your normal account, not the connector's.
Done whenEither it shows connected and NetSuite in Chrome shows your name and role, or you've established you don't have it and you're moving on.
What the connector adds

The review gets faster, and gains one extra check: each bill is tied back to the purchase order it is actually applied to, and to the engagement's billing history. That's what catches a duplicate payment or a missing intermediate application. Connected runs name the PO and history they checked against.

Everything else is the same either way — the figures, the support, the approvals all run through your browser and never touch the connector.

It isn't listed, and I have a NetSuite login

A normal NetSuite login is not connector access. The connector runs on a separate, Claude-enabled NetSuite account issued on top of your usual one — which is why people are surprised not to find it.

Check your email for a Compass invitation to add the NetSuite MCP connector, and contact IT if there isn't one. But don't wait on it. Finish setup and start using the plugin today; it picks the connector up on its own once you have it.

Got both accounts? Keep the browser on your normal one when approving — that's whose name goes on the approval. In the wrong one you'll see a queue that isn't yours, or a bill with no approve buttons.

Expect to reconnect it every few days

A connector session doesn't stay signed in for long. Plan on going back to Settings → Connectors and reconnecting every few days — the same two clicks. This is how the NetSuite connector behaves generally, for everyone using it for anything. Nothing about these plugins causes it or could prevent it.

A run tells you when it has lapsed, once, near the top — then carries on through your browser. The review still happens and the figures are still checked. What you lose until you reconnect is the purchase-order cross-check. Reconnect, run again, and it's back.

3

Install the plugins

  1. Click your profile at the bottom left, then Settings → Plugins.
  2. Click Add, then Add marketplace.
  3. Enter the repo: ssemwal-cdc/claude-sharables
  4. Check auto sync is ticked before you add it. It should be on by default — it's what keeps you on the current version.
  5. Add it. Both plugins appear in the list.
  6. Install the ones you need — netsuite-approval-review, procore-open-items-review, or both.
Done whenYour plugins appear in the installed list. Not showing up in a new chat? Restart your computer. Still nothing after that means the marketplace didn't add — remove it and add it again, checking auto sync is ticked.
Doing it in the terminal instead

Only if you already work in a command line. If that means nothing to you, ignore this — you're not missing anything.

claude plugin marketplace add ssemwal-cdc/claude-sharables
claude plugin install netsuite-approval-review@compass-claude-plugins
claude plugin install procore-open-items-review@compass-claude-plugins

You add the repo but install from the marketplace, which is why those names differ. It looks like a typo; it isn't. Inside a Claude chat the same commands work as /plugin … — and if a chat says Run /reload-plugins to activate, run that there.

Where your work is saved

Inside Downloads you get a NetSuite Approval Checks folder, a Procore Open Items folder, or both, created on the first run. Each holds your review history, the ids you confirmed, and the dashboard.

Choose Downloads every time — it's how a scheduled run knows whether it already ran today. Choose somewhere else, or rename or delete those folders, and the next run treats you as brand new and asks the setup questions again.

Nothing is shared between people; your ids and state stay local to your install.

Run it on a schedule

In that same chat from step 4, paste the block below for the plugin you set up — the whole block, in one message. Nothing to assemble, nothing to fill in.

Claude asks a couple of questions about timing, then gives you an Add to schedule button. Click it. Set up both plugins? Do it twice — paste one block, add its schedule, then paste the other.

A reasonable answer: first try 11am, retries 2pm and 4pm, weekdays only. Closed that chat already? Start a new Cowork chat, connect Downloads again, run the step 4 command once, then paste below.

It only runs while your computer is on and Chrome is open.

Claude works through your real browser session, so a scheduled run needs your machine awake, Chrome running, and you signed in. Laptop shut at 11am and nothing happens — it doesn't queue up and run later. That's what the extra fire times are for: if 11am misses, 2pm catches it, and if 11am succeeded the later ones see the day is done and stop.

Using it day to day

Once the schedule is running the review has already happened and the dashboard is waiting — provided your machine was on and Chrome open when it fired. You can also start one any time with the same command from step 4.

  1. Read the dashboard. Each item shows a verdict, the figures behind it, and a link to the record.
  2. Newest is at the top — most recent first on NetSuite, longest deadline first on Procore. Sort, filter or search if the queue is long: by amount, age, vendor or type. Both dashboards have the same controls and remember what you set.
  3. Step 1 — mark the ones you want to action. Nothing has left the page at this point, and you can change your mind on any of them.
  4. Step 2 — press Execute. There is a button at the bottom of the queue, and once you have marked anything a second one appears up in the header where you cannot miss it. Either one puts the instruction into the chat in one click.
  5. The approvals run through your own browser session, one record at a time.
Marking an itemThe buttons you'll see
NetSuite Approve, With notes, or Reject.
Procore Whatever that item's workflow step offers — usually Approve or Yes, and Reject or Revise and Resubmit. No "With notes", and a negative response asks you for a reason.
Every approval it makes says so. Approvals carry the note Approved by Claude in the audit trail. Type your own note for an item and yours is used instead, word for word. Rejection reasons are never filled in for you — it stops and asks.

If something goes wrong

A run stopped partway, or nothing was approved
What you seeWhat to do
It stopped after one item and reports a frozen tab or that a click didn't land Nothing was approved — it stops rather than guessing, and it won't click twice. The record tabs are open; click through those manually, or say "retry the batch".
It says the session expired Chrome was closed, or you were signed out of Procore or NetSuite. Reopen Chrome in your Compass profile, sign in to both, run it again. It never logs in for you.
It says the connector session expired Different thing — the NetSuite connector, not your browser. Reconnect under Settings → Connectors. The review still works meanwhile; it just skips the purchase-order cross-check.
An empty queue when you expected items Say so. A failed query and a genuinely empty queue look the same from the outside, and it should re-check through the browser rather than trust the first answer.

These are the failures actually seen in use. Anything else, tell Shivam rather than working around it.

Keeping it updated

With auto sync on, there's nothing to run. Fixes reach you on their own and apply on your next computer restart — so in practice you just have them the following morning.

Forcing an update now

Only if you need a fix immediately, or auto sync got switched off.

Press Win + R, type cmd, hit Enter. (On a Mac, open Terminal.) Paste these one at a time:

claude plugin marketplace update compass-claude-plugins
claude plugin update netsuite-approval-review@compass-claude-plugins
claude plugin update procore-open-items-review@compass-claude-plugins

Then restart your computer. The first command reports success whether or not anything shipped — only a changed version hash proves an update landed. The second needs the @compass-claude-plugins suffix or it says the plugin isn't found. It is.

What it will never do
  • Approve or reject on its own judgement. A verdict is a recommendation, nothing more.
  • Act on "approve everything clear." It requires named documents and will ask which.
  • Click without re-checking. It confirms the number, vendor and amount on the record immediately before each click.
  • Keep going after a problem. If an item can't be confirmed, the whole batch stops.
Questions, or something here that doesn't match what you see — tell Shivam. The plugins update from ssemwal-cdc/claude-sharables; every push is a new version.
